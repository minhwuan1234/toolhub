/Button.Primary/ *Give the screen one clearly identifiable primary action when the task needs an action.*
/Button.Secondary/ *Use a quieter outlined or neutral treatment for secondary actions.*
/Button.Label/ *Use a short verb or verb phrase that describes the action. Keep icon-only buttons accessible with a name.*
/Button.Size/ *Keep primary action targets at least 40px high or wide and leave enough space between adjacent actions.*
/Button.State/ *Represent default, hover, focus, disabled, and applicable loading states consistently.*
/Button.Destructive/ *Use a distinct warning treatment for destructive actions and require confirmation when the action cannot be undone.*
/Button.Prototype/ *In a UI/UX deliverable, a visible button describes the intended interaction; it does not claim the action works unless implementation is requested.*
