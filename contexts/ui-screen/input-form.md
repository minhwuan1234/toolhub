/Input.Label/ *Give each field a persistent visible label or accessible name; placeholder text is supporting copy, not the only label.*
/Input.Group/ *Group related fields and keep their labels, controls, help, and errors close together.*
/Input.Size/ *Use a comfortable control height and enough internal padding for readable text and pointer interaction.*
/Input.Type/ *Choose the control that fits the value: single-line input, multiline text area, search field, checkbox, or selection menu.*
/Input.Select/ *Use the Toolhub styled selection menu rather than a raw browser select appearance where the product already has a menu pattern.*
/Input.Validation/ *Show errors at the field that needs attention, in text the user can act on.*
/Input.State/ *Show focused, disabled, loading, and filled states when they affect understanding of the screen.*
